# The Wire — news website

## The problem
Staying informed is hard. There is too much information out there, and news sites are overwhelming.
I want everything in one place, summarized and easy to read.

## Who it's for
Me and my friends. It's not a business (for now).

## What it should have
- **Selectable topics:** stocks, crypto, programming and more.
- **Summarized news:** only the most important stories, 2–3 lines each.
- **"Read more":** if a story is interesting, it expands with more detail and a link to the original source.
- **Live price carousel** in each section: stocks in the stocks section, crypto in the crypto section.
- **Calendar of important talks and events** (conferences, product launches, company earnings, etc.).
- **Automatic daily updates.**
- **Chatbot** to ask questions about the day's news.

## Visual style
Futuristic, dark and clean, inspired by starknet.io: near-black background, white and grays, green/red only for prices.
(It used to be an "old stock exchange" look; changed on 2026-11-11. Full guide in `STYLE.md`.)

## How to start
Minimal first version: a few topics, a few stories per day, and the carousel.
If I use it every day, I'll add the calendar and the chatbot later.

## Reference ideas
- News Minimalist (news ranked by importance)
- TradingView widgets (price ticker tape)
- Binance WebSocket API (live crypto)
